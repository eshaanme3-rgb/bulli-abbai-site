Our dishes

Signature

Gida Masoori rice Kodi Pulav

Our signature preparation of fragrant rice and succulent chicken, layered with aromatic spices and cooked to bring depth, warmth, and balance to every serving.

Special Chicken Fry

Our biryani finished with the signature fry piece, layered with fragrant rice and slow cooked spices for a rich, complete plate.

Dehradun Chicken

Chicken fried with a bold Dehradun-style masala and finished with curry leaves, for a dark, intensely spiced bite.

Chicken Fry

Tender chicken coated in a finely seasoned spice blend and cooked to a beautifully crisp finish, delivering a bold flavour with every bite.

Dehradun Fish

Fish fillets coated in a bold Dehradun-style spice crumb and fried until crisp, finished with lemon and onion on the side.

Chicken 65

Succulent pieces of chicken, delicately seasoned and fried until crisp, finished with a vibrant blend of spices for its unmistakable character.

Pachi Mirchi

Pulav layered with fresh green chilli and herbs, for a sharp, fragrant heat alongside the signature chicken.

Karivepaku

Pulav finished with curry leaf and a herb-forward masala, bringing an earthy, aromatic character to every grain.

Mughalai

A milder, richly spiced pulav in the Mughlai style, with tender chicken folded through fragrant basmati rice.
